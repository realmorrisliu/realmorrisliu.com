## September, on Repeat

September is a particularly good month to listen to _September_ by **Earth,
Wind & Fire**.

I recently bought an **Audio-Technica AT-LP70X** turntable and a **Marshall
Woburn III**. I am only starting to choose my first records, so there is no
collection yet. For now, I am looking forward to finding out what deserves to
become the first few.

Eventually, the turntable, speaker, and records will all have their own place in
the living room.

## A Different Shape at Home

Since Jimmy passed away, the home has slowly started to change. The arrangement
of the rooms is no longer quite what it was when he was here.

I recently bought a reproduction cabinet in the style of **Pierre Jeanneret**.
It feels better than I expected, and it will become the place for the turntable,
the Woburn III, and whatever record collection grows from here. It is a small
change, but it gives the music a physical place in the room.

## Something Alive

I also want to bring more plants indoors.

The one I like most is the **olive tree** (_Olea europaea_), but I am a little
worried about pests and do not yet understand how to prevent or manage them
properly. I would rather learn first than bring one home and figure it out too
late.

So I started with a few **corkscrew willow** (_Salix matsudana_ 'Tortuosa')
branches and am keeping them in water. Once I understand plant care and pest
control better, I will look at olive trees again. For now, the willow is enough
to make the room feel a little more alive.

## Midnight at Work

Work has taken up most of the time left over lately. On most nights, I am still
at my desk around midnight.

It has also been a productive stretch. We have finished many of our goals ahead
of schedule, and I am proud of that. I just do not want midnight to become the
normal shape of every month.

## What's Next

I want to settle the new music corner, choose the first few records, and learn
enough about indoor plants to decide whether an olive tree can work here.

I also hope work becomes a little less intense before midnight starts to feel
normal.

The home is not going back to what it was. For now, I am trying to make the
version after Jimmy feel a little more alive.
