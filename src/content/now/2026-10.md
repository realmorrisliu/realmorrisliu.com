## The Road Across Guangxi

We have been to Guilin and Yangshuo many times over the years, so for the
October holiday, my wife and I decided on a different road trip route across
Guangxi: **Wuzhou**, **Liuzhou**, **Hechi**, **Nanning**, **Chongzuo**, and a
quiet overnight stop in **Yulin** on the way back.

Wuzhou, Liuzhou, and Nanning were mostly an unhurried crawl of eating and
drinking—which in practice meant an endless succession of rice noodles and cups
of milk tea. In Hechi and Chongzuo, the trip shifted outward to the landscape:
winding through massive karst caves and watching the rush of the Detian
Waterfall along the border.

After the intense pace of September, spending a week on open highways and
mountain roads was genuinely fun, and a welcome pause from routine.

## Breathing Room, and Alan

Work started easing up during the final week of September. The midnight hours at
my desk did not turn into the permanent shape of things, which came as a relief.

On the side, the **Jimmy** app has been quiet lately without much new progress.
**Alan**, on the other hand, is shifting gears: rather than just cleaning up
runtime internals and refactoring state in the background, I have started
pushing it toward being genuinely usable in everyday work.

## 11 Vinyls, 7 CDs

The music corner on the Pierre Jeanneret-style cabinet is largely settled now,
housing the **Audio-Technica AT-LP70X** turntable and the **Marshall Woburn
III**. I also added a standalone CD player to the setup.

What started in September as an empty shelf is now 11 vinyl records and 7 CDs.

On vinyl:

- **Daft Punk** — _Random Access Memories_
- **AC/DC** — _Back in Black_
- **Charlie Puth** — three albums
- **Queen** — _Greatest Hits_
- **Taylor Swift** — _1989_
- **David Tao** — self-titled debut ("The Yellow Album")
- **Bruno Mars** — two albums
- **Vince Guaraldi Trio** — _A Charlie Brown Christmas_

On CD:

- **ZUTOMAYO** (ずっと真夜中でいいのに。) — _Jinkou-gaku_ (沈香学)
- **Kessoku Band** (結束バンド) — _Bocchi the Rock!_
- **Jay Chou** — _Greatest Works of Art_
- **Sheena Ringo** — _Muzai Moratorium_ (the one with "Marunouchi Sadistic")
- **Hikaru Utada** — two albums
- **Official HIGE DANdism** — _Mixed Nuts EP_

Dropping a needle or sliding a disc into the tray has made listening to music
feel tactile and deliberate again.

## The Second Attempt at Green

The corkscrew willow branches I kept in water last month ended up getting moldy,
so I threw them out.

Instead of water cuttings, I bought proper potted plants, and they have been
growing noticeably well: a **Philodendron selloum** (春雨) from Walmart, a
flowering **begonia** (海棠), and an **Abarema clypearia** (密叶猴耳环) with its
fine, feathery leaves. A few days ago, I also picked up a **sweet osmanthus**
(_Osmanthus fragrans_ / 四季桂) from Sam's Club.

All of them are thriving, and the living room finally has something green that
actually feels alive.

## Pausing the D10

The **Aceoffix D10** commute has been on pause for a stretch because of the
weather. I am planning to get back on the bike and resume daily rides to work
later in October once the weather settles.

## What's Next

I still have not figured out what I want this autumn to look like. Trying to
direct and sustain my attention has felt harder than usual lately, almost
resistant to planning.

Maybe that is just what happens after an intense sprint. For now, I will take it
one step at a time: nudging Alan into something I can actually rely on, watering
the plants, and working through the stack of records.
