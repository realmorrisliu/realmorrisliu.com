## Design Context

### Users

CCI readers who want to understand the evidence, scoring assumptions and progress toward a global Top 32.

### Brand Personality

Clear, restrained and trustworthy. The creator selected a concise research report with conclusions before evidence.

### Aesthetic Direction

Keep the site's existing monochrome editorial typography. Use spacing, alignment and progressive disclosure to clarify dense research; preserve access to the underlying sources.

### Design Principles

- State the research conclusion before its supporting detail.
- Distinguish reviewed cases, conditional scores and global ranking eligibility.
- Keep reading lines comfortable and comparison tables easy to scan.
- Retain evidence, uncertainty and keyboard access on small screens.
